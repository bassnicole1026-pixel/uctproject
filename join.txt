Collatz Project — A step-by-step effort to prove that every positive integer eventually reaches 1 under the Collatz map.

Current milestone:
- A strict-descent proof for odd n ≡ 1 (mod 4).
- A two-step strict-descent proof for n ≡ 3 (mod 16).
- Exact computational verification for every starting value from 1 through 100,000.

The full Collatz conjecture has not been proved by this project. Finite computation and partial descent results are not a universal proof.
