Collatz Project — a step-by-step effort to prove that every positive integer eventually reaches 1 under the Collatz map.

Current pure-math milestone:
- The exact odd-only transition and cycle identities are recorded in the research roadmap.
- Nontrivial odd cycles with exactly one or exactly two high-valuation blocks are excluded.
- For exactly three high-valuation blocks, the quotient-one case is excluded and the total run length satisfies T ≤ 34.
- Exact modular and rational-product arguments exclude T=5 and T=10, leaving T ∈ {17, 22, 29, 34} unresolved.
- Cycles with four or more high valuations, and the general descent problem, remain open.

The strict estimate of completed proof is 0%: these partial results do not prove the Collatz conjecture. The active work uses exact identities and pure arithmetic; finite orbit computation is not part of the proof strategy.
